# Venue

## First choice: ACM Digital Government: Research and Practice (DGOV)

- Site: https://dl.acm.org/journal/dgov
- Gold open access, interdisciplinary
- Editors: Soonae Ae Chun, Beth Simone Noveck
- Scope: technology in public institutions, AI in government, practice papers that show a real problem and how it was solved
- Why it fits: Similaridade is an operational ombudsman tool, not a new clustering algorithm. DGOV has a practice track for that

Article type to aim for: **Practice paper** (problem faced, method used, measured result, what remains open).

## Alternatives

| Journal | Why | When |
