# Venue

## First choice now: ACM Digital Government: Research and Practice (DGOV)

- Site: https://dl.acm.org/journal/dgov
- Gold open access. Practice papers sit next to research papers.
- Scope: technology in public institutions, AI in government, a real problem and how a public body ran it.
- Why it is the right first door: Similaridade is an operational ombudsman tool, not a new clustering theorem. Romberg and Escher already used DGOV to review NLP on citizen text, and they list duplicate detection as one of the three evaluation jobs (DOI 10.1145/3603254). A Ceará practice paper answers that review with one Portuguese stream and an honest limit.
- Article type: **Practice paper** (problem, method, measured \(\rho\), what remains open).
- What still blocks a send: COUVI labels, a filled parameter manifest, and a written leftover-LLM host.

Format: `acmart` `acmlarge` + `\acmJournal{DGOV}` in this repo. Review copy uses `\documentclass[manuscript]{acmart}`.

## Why not the stronger method journals yet

| Journal | Why it looks tempting | Why not this draft |
|---|---|---|
| *Government Information Quarterly* (Elsevier) | Highest digital-government IF; policy and citizen outcome | Wants time-to-resolution or a labeled precision, not only \(\rho\) |
| *Information Processing & Management* | Home of TF-IDF; IR evaluation culture | Would reject a clustering paper without gold and without \(\varepsilon\) |
| *IEEE TKDE* / *Information Systems* | Duplicate-record and linkage audience | We do not propose a new linker; Fellegi–Sunter is the frame, not the claim |
| *Journal of Information and Data Management* (SBC) | Rabbi et al. 2026 published the closest Portuguese duplicate work there | Fine as a second, regional door; weaker international read |
| *Transforming Government* (Emerald) | Operations and process | Use if DGOV page charges or wait time hurt |
| *IEEE Access* | Fast methods note | Only if the team wants parameters, not the Ouvidoria case |
